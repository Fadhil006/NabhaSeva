🏥 Patient Portals: The Essence

Definition
Healthcare organizations use patient portals to provide convenient and secure 24/7 access to:

Personal Health Information (PHI)

Medical advice and assistance

A self-service patient portal improves:

Communication with healthcare providers

Patient engagement in treatment processes

Key Functionality

Doctor visit arrangement

Disease-specific modules

Telehealth

Payments

Seamless integration with IT environment

🌟 Key Features of Patient Portals
1. Patient Access to Personal Health Information

Encrypted and password-protected login

Health history, diagnoses, allergies, chronic conditions

Lab test results & discharge summaries (view/download)

Treatment, procedures, and medication plan

Personalized lifestyle recommendations & educational materials

2. Doctor Visit Arrangement

Online appointment scheduling

View upcoming appointments & re-schedule visits

Detailed visit history

Pre-visit form completion online

Automated appointment reminders (SMS or portal notifications)

3. Secure Patient-Doctor Communication

Messaging between doctor and patient

Video consultations (telehealth)

Secure access to mental/behavioral health records

After-visit patient surveys

Participation in research via dedicated clinical trial portals

4. Payment & Insurance Management

Access to bills and benefits explanations (with billing codes, e.g., CPT)

Online bill payment

Insurance coverage cases list

5. Online Patient Requests

Prescription refill requests

Medical procedure requests

Update requests (contact, billing, or health records)

6. Portal Administration

Granting portal access to new users

Editing patient health and contact information

Uploading clinical documents from other healthcare providers (e.g., CCDA visit summaries)

7. Patient Profile Settings (New Feature)

Patients can update personal details such as:

Mobile number

Address

Email

Patient ID remains fixed and cannot be changed

Ensures data accuracy and up-to-date communication channels
